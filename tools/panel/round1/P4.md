# P4 - Knowledge-management specialist

Owns: the intake contract, the frontmatter, the connection to the Obsidian wiki. Ran T03 and T04.

## Verdict

The vault step is technically careful. The notes are valid UTF-8, the frontmatter values are quoted so
a colon in a title cannot break the YAML, the file name follows the profile's pattern, and the two
guarded folders are respected: nothing was written in `wiki/`, and `raw/fieldwork` and `raw/legacy` were
not touched. That is the hard part and it is right. What is missing is the other direction of the link.
A note cannot be traced back to the radar record, the radar cannot see the note, the `ingested` flag is
written once and never read, and two promised fields are always empty. The connection is a one-way
delivery, not a round trip, and the design documents call it a round trip.

## Findings

| # | Sever | Feature | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S1 | F16 | The radar never shows whether a note exists for an item. 26 notes were written this morning and the radar cannot say so. | No note field in the item record; the item view has no wiki section. | Write `wikiNote`, `wikiWrittenAt` and `wikiIngested` into the item, and render them. |
| 2 | S1 | F17 | A note carries no identifier of the radar record, so nobody can walk from the wiki to the radar. | The frontmatter of a written note has title, source, authors, year, published, created, venue, doi, description, intake, ingested, radar_score, radar_themes, xray_confidence, thinker_candidates, tags. No radar id. | Add `radar_id` and `radar_url` to the contract, and use them in the wiki page's own links. |
| 3 | S2 | F18 | A rewritten note loses the link between the old and the new version. `--rewrite` overwrites in place, which is fine for the pipeline but bad for the corpus history. | `tools/vault_notes.py` writes the same file name; the vault is not under version control. | Write a change line into the note, or write `radar_updated` and keep the old body under a heading. |
| 4 | S2 | F19 | `xray_confidence` and `thinker_candidates` are declared in the contract and always empty. A pipeline that reads them learns to ignore them. | 26 of 26 notes have both empty. | Fill them from the abstract, or remove them from the contract and say why. |
| 5 | S2 | F16 | The `ingested` flag is never updated, so the radar cannot tell a delivered note from a wiki page. | No tool writes `ingested: true`; the vault is not read by any tool. | Read the note back before the next run and set the flag from what is found. |
| 6 | S3 | F19 | The note does not record which theme *names* it belongs to, only the codes, so a wiki reader sees `[par, dec]` with no legend. | `radar_themes: [par, dec]` in every note. | Add the theme names next to the codes. |

## What would change my mind

Findings 1 and 2 are the pair that matters: one identifier field in the note and one field in the item,
plus a small function that reads the intake folder at the start of a run. That is a morning's work and
it turns a delivery into a round trip. I would refuse any redesign that leaves the `ingested` flag
write-only, because a flag nobody reads is a lie in the data.
