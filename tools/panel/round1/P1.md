# P1 - The owner (lector, 20 minutes each Monday)

Brief received: PROJECT.md, the eight themes, the five live projects, the wiki contract, the feature
list F01-F25, the tasks T01-T14, and the measurements of the panel.

## Verdict

I cannot do my Monday review in this radar. I can read what the last run found, and I can read the
digest, and both are good. What I cannot do is finish the loop: decide what matters, and get that
decision back into the radar. The triage is the whole point of a personal watch, and there is no way to
record it. Add to that: the abstracts are not in the records, so I cannot check a `why` without leaving
for the publisher; the wiki side is invisible, so I cannot see whether the note I depend on exists; and
213 candidates wait in a queue I cannot read. The radar is a reader today, not an instrument.

## Findings

The calibration the whole scoring method depends on cannot happen. | No tool writes the status: `grep` over `tools/*.py` shows only `promote.py` writing `status: "new"`. 151 items sit at `new`. | A triage control in the item view that writes a triage file, plus `tools/triage.py` that applies it to `items/`. |

## What would change my mind

Two of these are one-line fixes (findings 2 and 6). If the abstract and the triage existed, my verdict
would move from "reader" to "instrument" in a single round. Finding 3 is the one I would put first: the
queue is where the radar's value is, and it is invisible.
