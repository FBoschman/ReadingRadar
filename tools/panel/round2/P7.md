# P7 - Subject expert: urban innovation and education sciences (round 2, on the R1 build)

## Verdict

The two false-positive classes are closed: a title that begins with "Correction" or "Erratum" is dropped by the harvest and refused at promotion, and the collection no longer holds an item below its own score floor without the reader's explicit approval. The correction notice that I found in the sample is gone. The reason field is now backed by an abstract for two thirds of the collection, which changes the reading experience: I can check a claim instead of trusting it.

My remaining objection is not about the features but about the instrument's own learning. The radar can tell me that I skipped five `ai` items and kept none, and it cannot act on that. For a watch whose whole purpose is to spend less of my attention, this is the highest-value thing still missing. My second point is smaller: the score scale still clusters high. With 149 undecided items and a floor of 3, the difference between a 3 and a 4 is doing the work that the scale should do.

## Findings

| # | Sev | Feature | Finding | Evidence | Fix |

| 1 | S2 | F12 | The radar knows what I skipped and does nothing with it. In a personal watch, that is the one feature that saves time every week. | The calibration card; no weights anywhere in the data. | Let the reader accept a proposed weight or exclusion, write it into the profile, and show the next run's counts against it. |
| 2 | S3 | F07 | The score distribution still clusters: 30 items at 5 in the earlier collection, 149 items undecided. | Manifest counts. | Write in `PROJECT.md` what a 5 means, and re-score the 5s that do not meet it. |

Finding 1. I would trade two of the new features for it, because it is the difference between a radar that reports and a radar that narrows.
