# P3 - Research librarian and review methodologist (round 2, on the R1 build)

## Verdict

The honesty problems are repaired, and that matters more than the features. The journal line now says how many papers the journal published and how many the sweep returned, so nobody can read "20 of 20 swept" as coverage. The provenance of a reason is visible: whether an abstract was read, from which source, and how exact the date is. The policy in the profile is enforced by the validator, with an explicit allow-list for the legacy records, and a correction notice is no longer filed as a paper. What remains is a judgement question rather than a defect: about a third of the collection has no abstract from either source, so those reasons rest on a title. The records say so, which is the correct behaviour, but a reader who cites from them takes a risk. I would prefer a score ceiling for an item with no evidence at all.

## What would change my mind

Nothing here blocks me. My two earlier S-level objections (coverage reported as success, and provenance absent) are both met. I would raise finding 1 in the judgement rules rather than in the interface.
