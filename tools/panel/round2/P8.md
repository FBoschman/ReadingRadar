# P8 - Maintenance engineer (round 2, on the R1 build)

## Verdict

The two findings I would not sign off without are repaired. **A silent stop is now visible**: the cycle writes `data/meta/status.json`, the page shows a banner when the last run failed and when the data is older than ten days, and `sourceHistory.json` keeps the sources of each run. **The queue is bounded**: a candidate that no run promotes is dropped after four runs or thirty days, and the drop list is written to `data/inbox/expired.json`. The window is capped at fourteen days, so a long gap cannot flood a run, and the journal sweep now pages instead of truncating at ten rows.

Two operational notes stay open, and I accept both with the risk written down. The task still pushes to a public repository unattended, which the consolidation refused to change for a good reason, and the log has no rotation and no trend view of its own.

## What would change my mind

Nothing blocks me. The instrument now tells the truth when it stops, and the queue cannot grow without limit. Both were the difference between a tool that survives a year and one that rots quietly.
