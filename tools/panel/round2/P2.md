# P2 - Information-retrieval researcher (round 2, on the R1 build)

## Verdict

The two findings that mattered are repaired. **The discourse id is now a hash of the normalised URL, and the harvest refuses a candidate whose normalised URL or title already exists**, so the duplicate class is closed. **The journal sweep pages through the journal's own total and records how much it returned**, so the recall claim is now a number instead of an assumption. The search reaches the abstract, the caveat, the theme names and the date. Two weaknesses remain, and both are about the second half of the pipeline: the queue cannot be promoted, and the ranker still learns nothing from the reader. I also note a new, honest limit: the theme queries are capped at ten rows each, which is a relevance-ranked sample and not a coverage measure. The run report should say so, next to the journal numbers.

## Findings

| # | Sev | Feature | Finding | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | S2 | F12 | No relevance feedback reaches anything. The calibration view computes what the reader kept and skipped, and no weight, filter or ranking follows from it. | `renderCalibration` is display-only; the harvest reads no weights. | Turn the view into a decision: accept a proposal, write a weight or an exclusion into the profile, and let the harvest read it. |
| 2 | S2 | F23 | The queue can be read and marked but not promoted, so the 213 candidates cannot become items without leaving the page. | No promote path in the interface; `promote.py` works from a file. | One command from the queue: mark keep with a reason, then apply. |
| 3 | S3 | F13 | Theme queries remain a sample of ten relevance-ranked rows. That is a reasonable watch, but it is not coverage, and the report does not distinguish the two. | `harvest.py` passes `rows=limit` for the theme queries. | Record the theme rows as a sample in `lastRun`, and keep the coverage language for the journal watch only. |
| 4 | S3 | F05 | The theme filter is still single-valued. | `state.theme` holds one value. | A multi-select filter, or a saved view. |

## What would change my mind

If findings 1 and 2 are built, this radar has a complete loop: harvest honestly, rank with the reader's own signals, and let the reader promote. I would then raise F12 from a refusal to a pass, and my pooled verdict would follow.
