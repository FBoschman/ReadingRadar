# P6 - Research-data and reproducibility reviewer (round 2, on the R1 build)

## Verdict

The two S1 defects are repaired and proven. `ITEM_FIELDS` now carries `abstract` and `abstractSource`, and the repair was applied to the collection: **105 of 160 items carry an abstract, all from OpenAlex, and the promotion path can no longer drop the field.** The discourse id is a hash of the normalised URL, and the harvest refuses a candidate whose normalised URL or title already exists, so the duplicate class is closed; the existing pair was merged. The validator now enforces the written policy with an allow-list, and the audit trail gained `status.json` and an append-only `sourceHistory.json`. My finding on the manifest was accepted: the field is `generatedAt` and the kit's provenance is kept as `firstExport`.

What I refuse is the calibration. The reader's decisions are counted in a view and used nowhere. My earlier point stands in a sharper form: **the criterion as I wrote it cannot be met by this architecture**, because the scoring is a judgement by a person or an agent, not a function. So the redesign must first restate the criterion — "a decision becomes a proposal the reader can accept in the profile, and the next run shows the effect" — and then build the mechanism. Until that exists, the loop is open at one end.

I also confirm the adversarial note from round 1, unchanged: the eight reviewers are one model, the 200 cells are correlated, and the interval is a consistency check on a frozen specification. The score report must say so above the table.
